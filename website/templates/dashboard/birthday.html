Birthday

Track member birthdays and celebrate together. Members set their birthday with /birthday set in Discord.

Announcement Channel

Channel where birthday messages are posted daily at midnight UTC.

Birthday Role

Role automatically assigned to members on their birthday.

How It Works

1. Enable birthdays and select an announcement channel.

2. Members use /birthday set in Discord to register their birthday.

3. Prowl announces birthdays daily at midnight UTC and assigns the birthday role.

4. Use /birthday upcoming to see who's celebrating soon.
