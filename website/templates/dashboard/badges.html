How It Works

1. Badges are earned automatically based on member activity.

2. Message badges track total messages sent in the server.

3. Voice badges track total time spent in voice channels.

4. Tenure badges track how long a member has been in the server.

5. Special badges include First Member and Server Booster.

Use /badges in Discord to view your badges.
