About us

Small team, big impact.

We build bots we'd want to use ourselves.

WhiteBots is a small development studio focused on building high-quality Discord bots and community automation tools. We started with a simple idea: server owners shouldn't have to settle for generic, unreliable bots.

Every bot we build is custom-tailored to the server it serves. No bloat, no unnecessary features — just the tools you need, working exactly how you want them.

We also develop and maintain Prowl, our flagship moderation and automation bot, trusted by communities of all sizes.

Whether you need a brand-new bot, improvements to an existing one, or ongoing maintenance, we're here to help.
