The latest improvements to Prowl.

2026

Dashboard refresh - Moderation quick-actions now run instantly through a direct link to the bot (no waiting on the queue), with automatic fallback when the bot is offline. The sidebar shows your display name, toasts got a smoother entrance, and the master toggle style is consistent across every feature page. Member lists and activity feeds are cached locally so pages load faster. The OAuth2 panel was removed from the dashboard.

Alpha dashboard - Moderation, Logs, AutoMod, Leveling, Automation, Raid Protection, Verification, Tickets and more are now configurable from the web dashboard. Sign in with Discord and manage your server from anywhere.

Continuing to build out the platform - more coming soon.
