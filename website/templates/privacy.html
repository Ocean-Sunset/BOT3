1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Prowl (“Prowl”, “we”, “us”, “our”) collects, uses, stores, and shares personal data when you use the Prowl Discord bot, the web dashboard available at prowlbot.xyz and its subdomains, and all related services (collectively, the “Service”).

Prowl is operated from France and processes personal data in accordance with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (“GDPR”) and other applicable privacy laws. We act as the data controller for the processing described in this Policy.

2. Information We Collect

Dashboard account data. When you sign in to the dashboard through Discord’s OAuth2, we receive and store your Discord user ID, username, display name, and avatar. We also record the time your account was created and your most recent login.

Server configuration data. When Prowl is added to a Discord server, we store the server’s ID, name, icon, and the configuration settings you choose for enabled features (for example: moderation settings, leveling settings, verification settings, ticket settings, autoresponder rules, and social alert settings), including the IDs and names of channels and roles you select.

Member data. For servers that use member-facing features such as moderation, member management, or leveling, we store a snapshot of basic member profile information as provided by Discord: user ID, username, display name, avatar, and join date. This data is used to display member lists, leaderboards, and moderation interfaces in the dashboard.

Moderation and activity logs. When moderation features are used, we store logs containing the action taken (for example: ban, kick, mute, warn), the IDs and names of the members and moderators involved, any reason provided, and a timestamp. We also store aggregate activity statistics such as member counts and message counts over time. These statistics are counts only and do not include message content.

Leveling data. For servers with leveling enabled, we store each member’s experience points (XP) and derived level within that server.

Technical data. Like most web services, our hosting and content delivery providers transiently process basic technical data such as IP addresses, browser type, and device information for security, abuse prevention, and reliability purposes.

Verification codes. When a server uses captcha-based verification, we temporarily store short-lived, single-use verification codes needed to complete the verification flow.

3. Information We Do Not Collect

  • We do not read, store, or log the content of your Discord messages or direct messages. Activity statistics are limited to aggregate counts.
  • We do not collect or store your Discord password. Authentication happens exclusively through Discord’s OAuth2.
  • We do not collect payment information, as the Service is currently free.

4. How We Use Information

We use the information described above to:

  • operate and provide the features you or your server have enabled;
  • display your servers, members, and settings in the dashboard;
  • authenticate you and maintain the security of your session;
  • execute moderation, verification, leveling, and automation actions at the direction of your server’s staff;
  • detect and prevent abuse, fraud, and attacks against the Service;
  • monitor reliability and improve the Service; and
  • respond to support requests.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not use it for advertising.

5. Legal Bases for Processing (GDPR)

Where the GDPR applies, we rely on the following legal bases:

  • Performance of a contract — processing needed to provide the Service you use, such as storing your server’s settings and executing the features you enable (Article 6(1)(b)).
  • Legitimate interests — processing needed to keep the Service secure and reliable, prevent abuse, and improve it, provided those interests are not overridden by your rights (Article 6(1)(f)).
  • Consent — for optional features where we ask for your consent, which you may withdraw at any time (Article 6(1)(a)).
  • Legal obligation — where processing is necessary to comply with a legal requirement (Article 6(1)(c)).

6. Cookies and Local Storage

Session cookie. The dashboard uses a single essential session cookie to keep you signed in. It is transmitted only over HTTPS, is not accessible to browser scripts, and expires automatically (24 hours).

Local storage. The dashboard stores small preferences in your browser’s local storage (for example, sidebar appearance and the last server you viewed). This data never leaves your device and can be cleared at any time from your browser settings.

We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

7. Sharing and Third-Party Services

We share personal data only with the following categories of recipients, each acting under its own terms and privacy policy:

  • Discord — the Service interoperates with Discord’s API; data you make available through Discord is processed in accordance with Discord’s Privacy Policy.
  • Infrastructure providers — Neon (database hosting), Vercel (application hosting), and Cloudflare (content delivery and security) process data on our behalf to operate the Service.
  • Captcha providers — if your server enables captcha verification, the verification challenge is provided and validated by Google reCAPTCHA or Cloudflare Turnstile, subject to their respective privacy policies.
  • AI providers — if you use AI features, the content you submit to those features is transmitted to a third-party AI provider solely to generate the requested output.
  • Font and asset providers — fonts and interface assets are served by Google Fonts and public content delivery networks, which may receive your IP address when your browser loads them.

We may also disclose information where required by law or to protect the rights, safety, and integrity of the Service and its users.

8. International Data Transfers

Some of our infrastructure providers are located outside the European Economic Area, including in the United States. Where personal data is transferred outside the EEA, we rely on appropriate safeguards recognised by the GDPR, such as standard contractual clauses or adequacy decisions, to protect it.

9. Data Retention

  • Dashboard account data is retained until you delete your account.
  • Server configuration and member data are retained while Prowl remains in your server and until deletion is requested; removing the bot from your server stops further collection.
  • Moderation logs are retained for server management purposes until the related server data is deleted.
  • Verification codes expire automatically within minutes of being issued.
  • Session cookies expire automatically after 24 hours.

10. Security

We protect data in transit using HTTPS with HSTS, store data with access controls, and sign session cookies to prevent tampering. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. If you become aware of a security issue, please contact us at support.prowlbot.xyz@gmail.com.

11. Your Rights and Choices

Where the GDPR or a similar law applies, you have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you;
  • have inaccurate data rectified;
  • have data erased (“right to be forgotten”);
  • restrict or object to processing;
  • receive your data in a portable format; and
  • withdraw consent at any time, where processing is based on consent.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at support.prowlbot.xyz@gmail.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the French data protection authority (CNIL) or with your local supervisory authority.

12. Account Deletion and Data Removal

You can delete your dashboard account at any time from the profile page. This permanently removes your account record and signs you out.

To remove your server’s data, remove Prowl from your server or contact us at support.prowlbot.xyz@gmail.com. Individual members of a server using Prowl may request deletion of their data through the server’s administrators or by contacting us directly.

13. Children’s Privacy

The Service is not directed to children under 13 (or under the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

14. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. When we make material changes, we will update the “Last updated” date at the top of this page and, where reasonably practicable, provide notice through the dashboard or our Discord server. Your continued use of the Service after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the updated Policy.

15. Contact

Questions about this Policy or your data? Contact us at support.prowlbot.xyz@gmail.com.
