Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 5, 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you use Prowl, we may collect server IDs, user IDs, channel IDs, role IDs, message counts, XP data, and configuration settings required to operate the bot's features. When you log into the dashboard, we may store your Discord user ID, username, and avatar.

2. How We Use Information

Collected information is used solely to provide and improve the service — such as storing your server's settings, tracking XP, logging moderation actions, and verifying accounts. We do not sell your data.

3. Data Storage & Retention

Data is stored on secure cloud servers. Configuration data is retained until you delete it or remove the bot. Moderation logs are retained for a reasonable period for server management purposes.

4. Third-Party Services

Prowl integrates with Discord, and may use third-party providers for features such as image generation and captcha verification. These services have their own privacy policies.

5. Account Deletion

You may delete your dashboard account at any time from the profile page. This removes your account record. To remove server data, remove the bot from the server or contact us.

6. Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach out through the Discord server or the contact information on the site.
